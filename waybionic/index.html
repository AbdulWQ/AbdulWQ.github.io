PCB work

7 PCBs, 2 jobs.

One base PCB manages the arm's safety, status, Arduino interconnect, and high-current power distribution. Six dedicated joint PCBs control nine stepper motors across the arm.

Motor Controller

Six dedicated 24 V controller PCBs are distributed across the arm's joints. Together, they command nine stepper motors, use encoder feedback for closed-loop positioning, and exchange motion and status data over CAN.

  • Board countSix joint controllers
  • ActuationNine stepper motors
  • NetworkCAN command and status

Base Station

One base PCB connects the arm Arduino to the joint network while coordinating safe 24 V / 25 A power distribution. It monitors system status and gives the hardware emergency stop a direct way to interrupt arm power.

  • Board countOne central base board
  • Power24 V / 25 A protected distribution
  • ControlArduino, safety, and status interface
